Questions Clients Ask

How long does a highlight appointment take?

It depends on your hair length, thickness and starting color. Highlights and balayage usually take longer than a single-process color, and we will give you a realistic time estimate during your consultation.


What is the difference between balayage and highlights?

Traditional highlights are applied in a more uniform pattern, while balayage is hand-painted for a softer, more blended grow-out. We will recommend the one that fits the look you want.


Can I go blonde if my hair is dark?

Often yes, though it can take more than one session to lift color safely. We check the condition of your hair first and plan a gradual path so it stays healthy.


Do you also offer straightening and smoothing?

Yes. We offer hair straightening and Brazilian keratin smoothing for clients who want sleek, frizz-controlled hair alongside their color.


What are your hours and payment options?

We are open Monday through Sunday from 10am to 7pm. We accept cash and credit cards.

Blonde Highlights

Blonde highlights add brightness and dimension around the face and through the length of your hair. We choose the tone, from soft champagne and warm honey to cool ash and icy platinum, based on your skin tone and the look you want.

Each section is placed carefully for an even, natural result, then toned so your blonde stays clean and luminous.

Balayage

Balayage is a hand-painted technique that sweeps color through the hair for a soft, sun-kissed finish with no harsh lines at the root.

It grows out gracefully, which means fewer root touch-ups and a look that changes with you between visits.

Partial Highlights

Partial highlights brighten the sections that frame your face and the top layers, giving you a lift without a full head of color.

They are a good choice for a quick refresh, for trying highlights for the first time or for keeping maintenance simple.

Lowlights and Dimension

Lowlights add deeper tones back into lighter hair to create depth, richness and contrast. They break up color that looks flat or too light and make blonde look more natural.

We can combine lowlights with highlights to build a multi-tonal color that has movement and shine.

Ombre and Hair Painting

Ombre blends deeper roots into lighter ends for a gradual fade, while hair painting uses freehand placement to create a custom, artistic pattern around your cut.

Both styles can be customized, from subtle shading to bold contrast, and both are shaped to suit your length and layers.
